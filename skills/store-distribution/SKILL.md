# Store distribution

- Per store, keep a checklist in the project docs: account owner, app
  identifiers, signing, required metadata (descriptions, screenshots,
  age rating, privacy details), review guidelines that apply.
- Generate screenshots from the game where possible.
- Anything needing the owner's account, payment or legal agreement is
  listed for them, with exact steps; never act in their name.
